# Renovation decisions

## 1. Decision log (one file, append-only)

Each entry:

```
2026-10-03  DECISION  Front door opens INTO the hall.
  why:       folds flat against the party wall; outward opening blocked the landing
  affects:   hall schemes B-J re-planned; nothing on the east wall beyond z 9.50
  source:    owner message / survey / purchase
  supersedes: 2026-09-14 "front door opens out"
  status:    open | decided | bought | built
```

Rules:

- **Latest explicit statement wins**, but record the one it replaced and why. Owners change their mind quickly; a log
  lets you see when you are going in circles.
- **Superseded is not deleted.** Mark old plans SUPERSEDED at the top and link the new one so nobody builds from it.
- Mark confidence: measured / photographed / assumed / told by the owner. Assumptions go to the measure-on-site list.
- When a purchase happens, flip status to **bought**, drop the alternatives from every tool, stop proposing new options,
  and update the model to the real product (a photographed texture beats a catalogue swatch).

## 2. Order of works (typical, check local practice)

1. Measure, finish the plan, decide layout and services (this toolkit).
2. Demolition and structural/opening changes.
3. Pipes, waste, gas (licensed), electrics first fix, ventilation ducts, heating.
4. Plaster / dry-lining and ceilings (decide lowered-ceiling heights with the light plan).
5. Screed / waterproofing (bathroom, balcony), then wall and floor tile.
6. Windows and external doors (often earlier: decide glazing type, frame colour, opening) and joinery frames.
7. Paint and wallpaper, skirting, floors (laminate after wet trades and dust).
8. Kitchen and wardrobes, second fix (sockets, lights), sanitary ware, doors, curtains.
9. Snagging list and cleaning.

Decide **long-lead and fixed items first**: windows, kitchen, tile (batch), radiators.

## 3. Budget

- Keep a table: category, item, shop, unit price, quantity, reserve, total, status.
- Show totals **per shop** (delivery, one order) and per room.
- Add contingency (10-15 % for renovations with hidden conditions) and a labour line per trade.
- You are not a financial advisor: give the arithmetic and trade-offs, not investment advice.

## 4. Handoff packets

| Trade | Give them |
|---|---|
| Carpenter / cabinet-maker | piece list (W × H × D, inside heights), hardware count, elevation with sockets, ceiling filler note |
| Tiler | layout per wall with joints, slab or tile count, no joints behind the hob/mixer, bought batch |
| Electrician | plan with socket heights, circuits, RCD, lighting groups, switch points, detector locations |
| Plumber | elevation with axes (waste 50, supply 55, wall tap 100), stack position, boxed wall sizes |
| Glazier | opening schedule (measured), type, frame colour, opening kind, which leaf opens, top light |

Print dimension chains from inner wall faces. Say which numbers are tape-measured.

## 5. Before every purchase

- Is the measurement a tape measurement, not a photo?
- Does the product fit the audit (door clear, aisle, services)?
- Is the reserve added and rounded to whole packs?
- Is it the same batch? Is the return policy known?
- Is the decision in the log?
