# Kitchen planning

Work from the **appliance list outward**. Pick appliances first, then layouts, then services.

## 1. Appliance states

Mark each appliance **on** (buying now), **opt** (reserve space, socket and pipes now, buy later) or **off**. This
avoids tearing out a wall for a dishwasher decided after the tiles. Typical list: hob + oven (or oven column),
fridge (built-in vs freestanding is a real choice), dishwasher, compact oven/microwave, hood, coffee machine, kettle,
air fryer, bread maker.

Sizes are typical per class: **check the bought model's manual** before ordering cabinets.

## 2. Layout rules that held up

- **One simple run beats clever ones.** A final preference was: full-height cabinet at one end with a single oven
  at 95-155 cm, then gas hob, dishwasher, sink, fridge at the doorway end. Variants differ only in end fillers
  (a 45 cm larder, a 30 cm pull-out, a 83 cm drawer bank).
- **Fridge at the door end**, hinged away from the passage, with a landing on its opening side.
- **Tall columns away from windows with curtains**; **hob mid-run**, never at a glazed end.
- **Hob landings ≥ 30 cm** each side; **sink-to-hob prep ≥ 90 cm**; dishwasher next to the sink; fridge ≥ 30 cm from the hob.
- **Aisle ≥ 100 cm** (120 for two cooks). Island overhang and stool width checked (stool ~40-45 cm per seat, overhang 25-30 cm).
- **Worktop 90 cm** → dishwasher niche 87 cm; standard plinth 10-15 cm; carcasses stop under the ceiling with a
  scribed filler closing the gap (ceilings are never level).
- **Hood 70 cm over gas** (60 over electric); duct route to the outside wall decided now. If wall cabinets stop at
  2.30 m, a cornice hides the duct.
- **Gas:** a methane detector 10-30 cm under the ceiling and ≥ 1 m from the hob driving a solenoid shut-off; flexible
  connection accessible in a 38-40 cm cupboard beside the hob. Follow local gas regulations and use a licensed installer.

## 3. Services plan

- **Worktop sockets:** 15 cm above the worktop, ≥ 30 cm from the sink bowl, ≥ 25 cm from the hob cut-out.
- **Built-in appliance sockets** go in the *neighbouring* cupboard (30 cm height for oven and dishwasher; in the cabinet above for fridge, hood, compact oven). Never behind the appliance, never under the sink.
- **Circuits (230 V, adapt to your code):** one 16 A each for oven, compact oven, dishwasher; fridge + hood + gas
  detector share one; two worktop circuits balanced by actual load; lighting 10 A; 30 mA RCD (type A) on each.
  `tools/quantities/calc.mjs` has `circuitLoad`.
- **Island power is optional.** No power = no floor chase, no Q7, and no compact oven in the island. Decide early.
- **Waste:** the sink waste run length matters when the stack is far (a 3 m run under a doorway threshold was flagged
  unverified). Keep slope and venting in mind.
- Read what is already in the wall (boxes, cables, water pipe) from photos of the bare wall; rectify along the wall
  using two known points; expect ±7 cm along and ±5 cm in height until a tape replaces them. Judge each point against the
  layout: fits / usable / must move.

## 4. For the cabinet-maker

Produce a piece list: every module, wall unit, filler, worktop, plinth and island with width × height × depth and
inside heights, plus a hardware count (drawer sets by cabinet width, hinges, lift-ups, pull-outs, handles, legs,
LED). Prefer fittings made from ordinary runners, hinges and brackets: "innovative" extras (plinth drawer with step
stool, pull-out board, dish rack, tip-out tray, bottle pull-out, flip-up bar leaf) are only worth it if a local
workshop makes them.

## 5. For the tiler

If the owner already has slabs (for example three 60 × 120 porcelain slabs), lay them lengthwise behind the worktop
(88-148 cm), choose cuts so **no joint lands behind the hob** and no piece is under 30 cm, cut the strip under the
hood from an offcut, and count slabs against what is in hand.

## 6. Checks to report

aisle, hob landings, sink-hob distance, dishwasher adjacency, fridge distance and landing, DW niche height, stool
width, overhang, passage to the balcony door, waste-run length, circuit loads, hob distance to a curtained wall.
